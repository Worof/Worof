## 👋 Who I Am

I sit at the intersection of **marketing, content, and machine learning**. With a computer engineering background, I've spent years leading content teams and running campaigns, and I now use data science and AI to make that work measurable and smarter.

- 📊 **Marketing data scientist:** customer segmentation (RFM), clustering, and analytics that turn behavior into strategy
- 🤖 **AI practitioner:** LLMs, RAG, and multi-agent systems with PyTorch, TensorFlow, and AWS
- ✍️ **Content lead:** managed a team of 3–5 across **200+ brands** and produced bilingual campaigns for GCC-market brands on TikTok and Snapchat
- 🌍 **Arabic–English bilingual:** I build and write for both audiences, and care about Arabic-language AI and storytelling

> *"Learning, building, and deploying, one model at a time."*
