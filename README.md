## 👋 Who I Am

I sit at the intersection of **marketing, data, and AI engineering**. With a computer engineering background, I lead content teams and build the data and automation systems behind modern marketing: segmentation models, RAG assistants, and multi-agent workflows that turn effort into measurable growth.

- 👥 **AI Content Team Lead:** led a team of 3–5 across **200+ e-commerce brands**, using performance data to guide messaging, channel allocation, and AI tool selection
- 📊 **Marketing Data Scientist:** RFM / RFM-D segmentation, clustering, and statistical validation, including rigorously reported negative findings
- ⚙️ **Marketing Engineer:** automating analytics, reporting, and campaign workflows with Python and AI agents
- 🤖 **AI Practitioner:** LLMs, RAG, and multi-agent orchestration, built to run locally with zero paid API cost

> *"Learning, building, and deploying, one model at a time."*

---

## 🔨 What I'm Building

I'm focused on **intelligent, agent-driven systems** that automate the repetitive parts of marketing and data work: retrieving data, reasoning over it with LLMs, and acting on it automatically. The goal is simple: **less manual effort, faster decisions, and growth that scales.**
